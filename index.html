About me

I am an aspiring Lighting Designer and Operator with a strong interest in theatre and live performance, especially work that supports storytelling and keeps performers as the main highlight during any show and the audience immersed.

Sheldon College

Graduating from Sheldon College at the end of 2025, I was part of the school's Sound and Lighting Team for four years. In my final two years, I moved into a paid role and took on a greater responsibility within the team, both technically and as a leader. I helped guide and support other student technicians while also becoming one of the main lighting programmers and operators for school productions and large events.

During my time at Sheldon College, I worked with a rig of around 100 fixtures, including dimmer-based systems, RGB LED PARs, moving lights, and gobos. I was regularly involved in rigging and setting up full lighting systems for weekly assemblies, often for audiences of about 1,500 people. These events were usually busked live, which helped me become confident making quick creative decisions under pressure.

I have also worked across other technical areas, including programming audio consoles, operating sound and AV playback for drama performances, and assisting with rigging and audio mixing (dLive 3500) for the senior production of High School Musical. Also in 2025, for the production of Matilda Jr., I programmed and operated the lighting adhering to the director's specifications. Beyond show operation, I carried out general equipment maintenance, including wireless microphone systems as well as lighting and hazer fixtures.

One of my biggest responsibilities at school was serving as the Backstage Technical Manager for the annual awards night (ASTA Arts Academies Awards), the largest event held in the venue each year. This required a full re-rig of the space within a single day, with bump-out typically completed in just 3 to 4 hours. Such experiences have taught me valuable skills regarding planning, teamwork and remaining calm in high-pressure situations.

Rockit Productions

Outside of school, I have both worked and performed with Rockit Productions, beginning as a volunteer during the 2023 show season as a member of the Technical Team and later being offered the role of Lead Lighting Operator. Rockit Productions typically stages 6 to 8 performances per production for audiences of around 150 per performance, using a lighting rig of roughly 40 fixtures. In this role I am responsible for programming and operating shows, often within tight timeframes: typically around three days of programming for a 90-minute production, with cue counts ranging from 170 to over 270 cues.

Consoles and approach

I primarily use ChamSys MagicQ (MQ70) and am confident in programming, busking, and live show operation on this platform. I have also completed independent training in grandMA3 to expand my knowledge of other industry-standard systems.

When designing, I focus on ensuring lighting works with every visual element on stage, from costumes to the set and scenery, keeping the performers clearly seen and supported. My goal is to use light to strengthen mood, guide attention, and help bring each performance to life.

I am always looking to keep developing my skills and take on new creative and technical challenges within the live production industry.
